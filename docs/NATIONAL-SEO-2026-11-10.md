# National city SEO release · 10 November 2026

The city directory expands from 11 to **56 published origin-planning guides**, adding 45 researched manuscripts. The complete public sitemap grows from 93 to **138 canonical URLs**. Coverage includes the major metro markets and selected regional gateways across 22 directory groups. JaisalGo continues to sell its existing local Jaisalmer arrangements: these pages do not advertise invented departures from other cities.

## What visitors and crawlers receive

- Each new city has a unique search title, description, opening answer, route analysis, two local-stay suggestions, group considerations, budget advice, arrival checks and an origin-specific FAQ. Source notes explain which facts the official references support. The shared booking terms remain consistent across pages.
- Every guide is server-rendered, has one H1, its own www canonical URL and English metadata, Article/WebPage/BreadcrumbList markup, source citations, a review date, a destination entity reference and an origin City entity with its state and country. No imaginary branches, local offices, reviews, hotel relationships or origin transport offers are added.
- The full directory exposes all 56 guides through ordinary HTML links and regional jump navigation. Homepage discovery remains limited to 16 representative guides plus the complete-directory link. Related-origin links are contextual and filtered through the publication gate; local package cards still use the actual published catalogue.
- Current city spellings and supported aliases resolve to a single canonical guide. New aliases include Aurangabad, Mysore, Mangalore, Cochin, Trivandrum, Calicut, Vizag and Allahabad. Chandigarh Tricity resolves to the reviewed Chandigarh planning guide. All city alias redirects are permanent 308s; alias URLs are excluded from the sitemap and anonymous analytics path allowlist.
- The existing sitemap automatically includes eligible guides and their real review dates. The curated `llms.txt` points to the directory. It is a supplemental discovery aid, not a promise of AI citation.
- Long names use a smaller responsive headline scale. Directory cards, navigation and contextual links wrap without horizontal scrolling; mobile links retain usable touch targets.

The ₹99 token, actual package estimates, supplier-confirmation conditions, booking forms, Razorpay verification, availability handling and operational WhatsApp alerts are unchanged. Origin tickets, gateway rooms and intercity transfers remain separate unless an accepted quote includes them. There are no new dependencies or APIs.

## Sustainable expansion and search intent

One canonical guide owns each origin-to-Jaisalmer planning intent, including genuine alternate names. Existing destination, itinerary and package pages continue to own their respective informational and commercial intent. The keyword map now uses each guide's authored title rather than a city-substituted metadata pattern.

The 103-market dataset remains a research backlog. **47 candidates are still unpublished**: their names alone do not generate pages, indexable placeholders or redirects to a different city. Nearby NCR markets are covered contextually by the Delhi guide until separate evidence warrants distinct content. Additional cities need useful local research, source scope, editorial review and real related inventory before publication. This release does not claim coverage of every Indian city.

The publication gate continues to exclude drafts, incomplete research, unsupported cities and guides without a published local experience. Related-city links use the same gate. Structured data contains no unsupported Offer, LocalBusiness office or aggregateRating. Search query variants retain existing canonical/noindex handling; booking, ticket and admin resources remain outside search discovery.

## Measurement and maintenance

The keyword inventory is qualitative: no search volume or demand estimate is invented. Regenerate the canonical map, redirect inventory, 103-market publication table and privacy-safe analytics path allowlist together with:

```sh
node --import tsx scripts/seo-keyword-map.ts
```

Use Search Console's existing verified www property and submitted sitemap to monitor discovery, indexing, selected canonical, origin-query impressions, clicks and CTR. The expanded sitemap uses the same endpoint; submitting hundreds of duplicate URL requests is unnecessary. Compare guide-to-package engagement with verified bookings in the existing consented reporting. Do not treat a checkout click as collected revenue.

For the first 30 days, check indexing and connection/source corrections. At 30–60 days, improve pages with actual impressions and weak CTR or engagement, and review closely related intents for consolidation. At 60–90 days, expand the backlog only where genuine search evidence and distinct planning value justify another guide. Recheck official gateways before seasonal travel changes; live schedules, fares and fixed transfer times always require dated provider checks.
