## Destination knowledge base
Official source: Rajasthan Tourism Jaisalmer page above. Jaisalmer Fort is a living heritage place; the city also contains Patwon, Nathmal and Salim Singh havelis and Gadisar Lake. Bada Bagh is about 6 km north; Kuldhara about 18 km outside the city; Tanot about 120 km away (official page distances; road routing varies). Desert National Park protects desert habitat including Great Indian Bustard habitat. Do not promise sightings. Do not conflate military memorials with access to the international border. Current permits, park access, attraction hours, ticket prices, airport services and travel times require rechecking before departure.

Editorial planning recommendations (not official durations): fort 2–3 hours, each haveli 45–90 minutes, lake 45–60 minutes, Bada Bagh about one hour. One day: city only. Two days: city plus an overnight desert excursion. Three days: slower city, dunes and culture. Four: add one long excursion with an early start and weather buffer. No precise drive time promised without route verification.

Seasonal guidance: cooler months usually suit outdoor exploration; winter desert nights need layers. Summer daytime heat can make dune activities unsuitable. Reconfirm camp operations, road conditions and weather. Transport: compare current rail, road and seasonal air schedules; arrange a documented arrival transfer. Old lanes and sandstone steps can be uneven; request exact step-free access, bathrooms and vehicle access, not generic accessibility promises. Families should discuss child seats, bedding, dietary needs and shorter outdoor blocks.

Pack water, sun protection, closed shoes, a warm layer, reusable bottle, medication and a torch. Ask before photographing residents or performances; follow temple dress and photography rules. Use licensed guides, agree fares and commissions beforehand, request a written inclusion list and receipt, and avoid unverified unsolicited pickup offers. Respect camel welfare, do not feed wildlife, stay on permitted tracks and avoid amplified music near villages. Fires only where lawful and authorized. Buy crafts directly with transparent pricing. Do not portray Kuldhara legends as established history.

Cuisine themes: dal baati churma, ker sangri and regional thalis; confirm allergens and kitchen practices. No individual café, artisan or hotel is endorsed without further checks. Photography suggestions are editorial: morning heritage lanes, low-angle dune light, lake reflections and evening rooftops where access is permitted. Ask before photographing people; night sky visibility depends on moon, dust and weather.

## Brand and art direction
Positioning: Your trusted guide to discovering Jaisalmer. Product must earn trust through factual context, honest price labels and useful choices, not unsubstantiated local-staff claims.
Three directions considered: (1) Royal Heritage, ornate deep indigo and gold: too ceremonial; (2) Desert Adventure, high-energy orange and sans-serif: too generic; (3) Golden City Field Journal: editorial serif, warm ivory, terracotta, clean modern UI. Select 3 for clarity and distinctiveness. Restrained sun/fort line logo; no copied assets.
Three hero concepts: fort panorama (selected; recognizable destination and architectural identity), dune sunset (strong mood but less distinctive), lake reflections (calm but underrepresents desert). Hero uses text beside/over documentary fort image with dark readability gradient; mobile has a dedicated taller composition and adjusted focal point.
